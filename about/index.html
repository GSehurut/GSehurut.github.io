About

Hi, I'm Goabaone

An actuarial analytics engineer working where actuarial science meets software engineering.

What I work on

I'm an Actuarial Analytics Engineer at The Fidelis Partnership in London. I build the actuarial data products, pipelines and internal tools that pricing, reserving and underwriting teams use every day — from FastAPI services and Microsoft Fabric pipelines to Power BI and Plotly dashboards.

I joined Fidelis as a Portfolio Analytics Analyst in 2024, working across Marine, Aviation, Space, Political Risk and Structured Credit. That meant migrating and optimising pricing models on Hx Renew, building the Realistic Disaster Scenario pipeline for quarterly exposure reporting, and putting analytics directly into underwriters' hands through an Excel add-in API.

How I got here

I studied Actuarial Science at the London School of Economics, then a Master's in Data Science at Heriot-Watt University, where my dissertation explored the languages of mathematics through the Lean theorem prover. Along the way I've passed seven Institute and Faculty of Actuaries exams and became a Graduate Statistician with the Royal Statistical Society.

What I enjoy most is turning messy, manual processes into reliable, tested systems that let actuaries and underwriters spend their time on judgement rather than spreadsheets.

Where I'm from

I'm originally from Botswana, at the heart of Southern Africa — home to vast desert landscapes and the Okavango Delta. There's no place quite like it.
